Why Jeevan exists

Our mission

Every year, thousands of people lose their lives simply because blood wasn't available in time. Jeevan was built to bridge this gap — connecting those who need blood with those who can give it. We believe that finding a blood donor should never be a struggle, especially in emergencies. Our goal is to make blood discovery fast, reliable, and accessible to every person in need.

Know the facts

Blood donation: myths vs facts

Let's clear up some common misconceptions about blood donation.

❌ Myth

"Donating blood makes you weak."

✅ Fact

Some people feel tired after donating. Rest, have a snack, and follow the care advice from the donation team.

❌ Myth

"You can catch diseases from donating blood."

✅ Fact

Donation centers use sterile, single-use equipment. If you have concerns, ask the donation team about their safety procedures.

❌ Myth

"Donating blood is very painful."

✅ Fact

You may feel a brief pinch when the needle is inserted. Let the donation team know if you feel uncomfortable.

❌ Myth

"I can't donate because I take medication."

✅ Fact

Eligibility depends on the medication and your health. Check with the donation center before you donate.

Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Common questions about blood donation answered.

Who can donate blood?

Eligibility depends on your age, health, and local guidelines. A donation center can confirm whether you are eligible.

How often can I donate blood?

Donation intervals vary by donation type and local guidelines. The donation team can advise when it is safe for you to donate again.

How long does the donation process take?

The actual blood collection takes about 10–15 minutes. Including registration and rest, the full visit takes around 30–45 minutes.

Is blood donation safe?

Donation centers follow safety procedures and use sterile, single-use equipment. Talk to the staff if you have any concerns.

What should I do before donating?

Have a healthy meal, drink plenty of water, get a good night's sleep, and avoid alcohol for at least 24 hours before donating.
