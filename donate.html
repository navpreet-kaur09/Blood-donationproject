✅ Are You Eligible to Donate?

✓ Age between 18 – 65 years
✓ Weight above 45 kg
✓ No tattoo or piercing in the last 6 months
✓ Not pregnant or breastfeeding
✓ No serious medical conditions
✓ Hemoglobin level above 12.5 g/dL

Why Donate Blood?

A few minutes of your time can mean a lifetime for someone else.

🩸

Save 3 Lives

One blood donation can save up to 3 lives. Your single donation is split into red cells, plasma, and platelets.

⏱️

Takes Only 15 Minutes

The actual blood donation process takes just 10–15 minutes. It's quick, safe, and supervised by professionals.

🔄

Blood Regenerates

Your body replaces the donated blood within 24–48 hours. You'll be back to normal in no time.
